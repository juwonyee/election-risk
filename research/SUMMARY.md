## What this says

1. **Expected impact is small for everyone: 0.05% to 2% of profit.** Three reasons: the President's veto holds
   until January 2029; the 2025 reconciliation law locked in SNAP, Medicaid, tax and enforcement funding; and
   many state races are not close.
2. **"If it happens" can be large** (Tesla +$3.4B, UnitedHealth $3.2B, Southern $1.0B), but mostly behind a veto.
3. **The election bites hardest where state bodies set prices directly**: utility commissions (Georgia PSC for
   Southern Company), gambling legalization and taxes (DraftKings), minimum wages for company-run workforces.
4. **The most surprising facts are often what is already locked in** (Tesla's emissions-credit income being cut
   by Congress already; Walmart's SNAP hit already in the numbers).
