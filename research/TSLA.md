# Tesla (TSLA): 2026 election exposure
Base: FY2025 revenue $94,827M, operating income $4,355M ([10-K, EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/0001628280-26-003952-index.htm), *verified*). H1 2026: revenue $50,623M, operating income $1,339M ([Q2 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026049270/tsla-20260630.htm), *verified*).
Concentration (all FY2025, 10-K, *verified*):
- **Geography:** US revenue $47,627M (50%), China $20,962M, other $26,238M.
- **Segments:** Automotive & services gross profit $13,292M. Energy generation & storage revenue $12,771M and gross profit $3,802M (29.8% margin).
- **Policy-linked lines:**
  - Automotive regulatory credits $1,993M (down from $2,763M in FY2024). That equals 46% of operating income, at almost no cost.
  - Section 45X manufacturing credits booked against cost of revenue: $565M automotive plus $1,120M energy, so $1,685M (39% of operating income).
- **2026 run-rate:** Regulatory credits were $380M in Q1 and $146M in Q2, so H1 was $526M versus $1,034M in H1 2025.

## Bottom line
- **In play (expected):** about **$86M/yr in gross terms (2.0% of FY2025 operating income)**. That splits into +$34M from channels where a D win would help Tesla and -$52M from channels that would hurt it, a net of about -$18M/yr. **If everything happens:** +$3.4B/yr on the D side (credits, tariff relief), or -$1.3B/yr on the R side (deeper 45X/48E cuts). These outcomes exclude each other. Almost every federal upside needs the President's signature before January 2029, so the probabilities of enactment are close to zero.
- **Locked in regardless:**
  - CAFE civil penalty set to $0 (OBBBA). Credit revenue has fallen from $2,763M (FY2024) to about $1,052M/yr annualized for 2026.
  - The 30D/45W/25E EV credits ended for vehicles acquired after 2025-09-30.
  - EPA repealed the GHG endangerment finding and the vehicle GHG standards (2026-02-12). This is in court, not on the ballot.
  - 45X ($1,685M in FY2025) and the 48E/45Y storage credits (through 2033) remain, with FEOC limits.
  - Section 232/301 tariffs remain. They ran above $400M/quarter in Q3 2025 including IEEPA tariffs, and Congress cannot undo them without a veto-proof vote.
- **Card fact:** In 2025, close to half of Tesla's operating profit ($2.0B of $4.4B) came from selling emissions credits to other automakers. Congress cut that business in 2025, and a Democratic Congress can't restore it while the President has a veto.
- **Strongest R-vs-D contrast:** Federal clean-car rules and credits. Democrats would restore CAFE penalties and EV credits, worth up to about +$2.4B/yr; under an R sweep, a second reconciliation bill could cut 45X and storage credits, worth up to about -$1.2B/yr. Neither side gets past the 2027-28 veto/filibuster math, so the expected values are small ($16-22M each).

### 1. Federal regulatory credits (CAFE penalty / GHG standards)
- **Election:** a D House plus a D Senate (D sweep about 61%, Kalshi chamber markets, as given in the brief).
- **Policy:** OBBBA (P.L. 119-21) set CAFE civil penalties to $0 ([Moneywise](https://moneywise.com/news/top-stories/tesla-carbon-credit-revenue-loss-trump-cafe-standards), [Fortune](https://dc.fortune.com/2025/06/10/tesla-revenue-regulatory-credits-senate-republican-budget-bill)). EPA rescinded the endangerment finding and all vehicle GHG standards on 2026-02-12, and that is being challenged in the D.C. Circuit ([PBS/AP](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/amp/politics/groups-sue-trumps-epa-over-repeal-of-rule-supporting-climate-protections), [Jones Day](https://www.jonesday.com/de/insights/2026/06/challenges-to-epas-repeal-of-the-2009-endangerment-finding)). NHTSA has proposed ending inter-manufacturer CAFE credit trading from MY2028 ([Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/nhtsa-proposes-overhaul-of-fuel-economy-standards-safe-rule-iii-for-model-years-2022-2031-passenger-cars-light-trucks.pdf)). A specific Democratic restoration bill was *not found*. Democrats opposed the provision in 2025.
- **Enactment:** 2% *estimated*. It needs the President's signature or a 2/3 override, and peak GOP crossover so far is 5 senators and 6 House members.
- **Size / exposure:** credit revenue fell from $2,763M (FY2024, *verified*) to $526M in H1 2026, about $1,052M annualized (*verified*). Tesla attributes the drop to "OBBBA" and to lower credit demand from other automakers. The split between US federal, state ZEV, EU and China credits is *not disclosed*.
- **Dollars:** if it happens, the recovery is ($2,763M - $1,052M) x 50-100% US-federal share *estimated* = $855-1,711M, midpoint about $1,300M. Credits carry "negligible" cost, so revenue ≈ operating income. Expected = $1,300M x 0.61 x 0.02 = **$16M**.

### 2. Consumer EV credits (30D/45W)
- **Policy:** 30D, 25E and 45W ended for vehicles acquired after 2025-09-30 ([IRS FS-2025-05 via Accounting Today](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/irs-provides-faqs-on-expiring-clean-energy-tax-breaks)).
- **Enactment:** 1% *estimated*. Same veto problem, plus it costs money.
- **Exposure:** Cox estimated Tesla's 2025 US sales at 577,097 ([CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2025/12/28/cox-automotive-forecasts-9-sales-decline-for-tesla-in-usa-in-2025/), *estimated*).
- **Dollars:** 577,097 x 70% eligible (*estimated*: Model 3/Y under the price caps) x $7,500 x 35% captured by Tesla through price/volume (*estimated*) = **$1,060M/yr**. Expected = $1,060M x 0.61 x 0.01 = **$6M**.

### 3. Tariffs (Section 232 autos/parts; Section 301 China, including battery cells)
- **Policy:** the 232 auto/parts tariffs and the 301 China/forced-labor tariffs stay in force. Congress has no disapproval route for them (brief context).
- **Exposure:** on the Q3 2025 call, the CFO put tariff cost at "over $400 million" for the quarter, split roughly evenly between automotive and energy. Energy sources its LFP cells from China ([AOL transcript](https://www.aol.com/finance/tesla-tsla-q3-2025-earnings-235047736.html), [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/tesla-q3-revenue-surges-28-to-28-1-billion-but-profits-slip-amid-400-million-tariff-hit-82965.htm)). IEEPA tariffs were struck down on 2026-02-20. The 10-Q says Tesla "may be eligible for a refund" but books nothing (*verified*). Q1 2026 included more than $200M of tariff benefit in energy ([energy-storage.news](https://www.energy-storage.news/teslas-energy-division-gross-margin-declines-19-despite-battery-storage-deployment-rebound/)). Tesla is moving cells to LGES Lansing, MI starting in 2H 2027 ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/03/17/tesla-lg-energy-solution-4-3-billion-lfp-battery-deal-megapack-confirmed/)).
- **Dollars:** $400M x 4 = $1.6B/yr at the 2025 peak, x 50-75% still in force after IEEPA (*estimated*) = $800-1,200M. Expected = $1,000M x 0.61 x 0.02 = **$12M**.
- **Market:** Kalshi KXTARIFFRATEPRC-27JAN01 "20% or above" trades at a 0.315 midpoint on 2,500 contracts (**thin**). The other strikes trade under 15 contracts, so they are not used.

### 4. Further cuts to 45X / storage credits (R sweep)
- **Election:** R sweep 11%.
- **Policy:** House Republicans have floated a second reconciliation bill; a specific 45X/48E text was *not found*. Current law ([Hogan Lovells](https://hlc.com/en/publications/house-passes-one-big-beautiful-bill-impact-on-energy), [MGO](https://www.mgocpa.com/perspective/obbba-energy-credit-changes/), [KPMG on Notice 2026-15](https://www.kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/02/notice-2026-15-guidance-energy-tax-credits-material-assistance-prohibited-foreign-entities.html)):
  - Storage 48E/45Y keeps full value for projects that begin construction through 2033.
  - 45X phases down at the end of the decade.
  - Both carry FEOC "material assistance" limits.
- **Enactment:** 15% *estimated*. Reconciliation needs only a simple majority and the President would sign, but the GOP was split in 2025 over cutting energy credits.
- **Dollars:** $1,685M (FY2025 45X, *verified*) x 50-100% removed (*estimated*) = $840-1,690M, midpoint about $1,200M. Expected = $1,200M x 0.11 x 0.15 = **-$20M**.

### 5. Federal EV registration fee
- **Policy:** the BUILD America 250 Act sets $130/yr for EVs and $35 for PHEVs, rising to a $150 cap ([Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/congress-proposes-annual-us130-fee-for-ev-drivers/)). The top T&I Democrat, Larsen, backs it ([E&E](https://www.eenews.net/articles/top-transportation-democrat-backs-ev-fee-in-highway-bill/)), so it is largely bipartisan. Highway programs are extended to 2026-12-11 (P.L. 119-103), so the fight lands in the lame-duck session ([CRS IG10101](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IG10101.html), [E&E](https://www.eenews.net/articles/hopes-fade-for-highway-bill-ahead-of-sept-30-deadline/)).
- **Enactment by end of 2027:** 30% with a D House, 40% with an R House (*estimated*). It is bipartisan at committee level, but Senate Democrats may resist.
- **Dollars:** the fee is about $900 present value over ownership, or about 2% of a $45K price. Elasticity of -1 to -2 (*estimated*) gives -2% to -4% volume. 577,097 x 3% x $5,825 gross profit/unit = **-$100M**. Gross profit/unit = (auto sales revenue $65,821M - cost $56,267M) / 1.64M deliveries (*verified* inputs). Expected = 0.90 x 0.30 x $100M + 0.10 x 0.40 x $100M = **-$31M**. The R-vs-D swing is only about $10M.

  - SB 168 "MyFirstEV" provides $135.5M of state money, matched by automakers: $3,500 off new EVs up to $50K MSRP ([Gov. office, 2026-07-13](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/07/13/as-trump-cedes-global-clean-car-race-to-china-governor-newsom-fights-back-with-instant-zev-rebates-for-first-time-buyers/)). Whether Tesla is eligible is *not found*. A 2024 draft would have excluded Tesla ([Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2024/11/25/tesla-excluded-from-ev-buyer-credits-in-california-proposal-newsom)).
- **Enactment:** 30% *estimated*, because the legislature has a D supermajority.
- **Dollars:** -$100M (range -$50M to -$150M, *estimated*: lost CA ZEV credit value plus rebate demand) x 0.038 x 0.30 = **-$1M**.

### 7. State AV / direct-sales rules
- **Texas:** SB 2807 requires a TxDMV permit for driverless operation and was fully enforceable on 2026-05-28. Tesla is among the authorized operators in Austin ([CBS Austin](https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/story/new-texas-law-tightens-rules-for-autonomous-vehicle-companies-including-waymo), [Spectrum](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/tx/austin/news/2026/05/12/changes-coming-to-avs)).
- **California:** Tesla holds no driverless DMV or CPUC deployment permit ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/18/what-tesla-can-and-cant-do-in-california-with-its-new-passenger-transportation-permit)). SB 1246 (Cortese) tightens robotaxi supervision ([SF Standard](https://sfstandard.com/2026/07/22/dave-cortese-sb-1246-robotaxi-qa/)).
- **Direct sales:** the 10-K notes some states bar manufacturer-dealers (*verified*, qualitative).
- **Dollars:** robotaxi revenue is not disclosed and likely immaterial in 2027. Not quantified.

## Locked in regardless
- **CAFE penalty set to $0 (OBBBA):** regulatory-credit revenue fell from $2,763M (FY2024) to $1,993M (FY2025) and $1,052M annualized for 2026. That is a cumulative loss of about $1.7B/yr of near-100%-margin income versus 2024.
- **30D/25E/45W EV credits:** ended 2025-09-30. Residential 25D ended 2025-12-31 (10-K).
- **EPA GHG endangerment repeal (2026-02-12):** courts decide this, not elections.
- **45X:** $1,685M in FY2025 ($565M automotive plus $1,120M energy). It stays through the decade, subject to FEOC limits on Chinese content.
- **48E/45Y storage credits:** retained for projects that begin construction through 2033. Customers capture most of the value.
- **Section 232/301 tariffs:** in force. Tariff cost was above $400M/quarter in Q3 2025 including IEEPA. A possible IEEPA refund is a court/CBP matter and is not booked.
- **NHTSA:** the proposal to end CAFE credit trading from MY2028 is executive action through January 2029.

## Biggest uncertainties
1. **The US-federal share of regulatory-credit revenue is not disclosed.** Channel 1's size ($0.9-1.7B) rests on an assumed split among federal, state ZEV, EU and China credits.
2. **P(enact) under divided government is estimated, not market-priced.** Under the veto, the D-side channels are essentially zero until 2029. The 2028 presidential race matters far more to Tesla than 2026.
3. **The size of the EV fee (channel 5) depends on an assumed demand elasticity.**
4. **Tariff costs after the IEEPA ruling and the move to Michigan cells are not disclosed.**
5. **Brand and political effects tied to Musk:** Kalshi KXMUSKCHALLENGERS-26 "at least 1" trades at 0.235, thin (vol 1,973). These effects are not a policy channel and are not modeled.
