# DraftKings (DKNG): 2026 election exposure
Base: FY2025 revenue $6,054.5M; GAAP operating income **-$15.8M** (EDGAR 10-K, accession 0001883685-26-000013: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1883685/000188368526000013/0001883685-26-000013-index.htm). GAAP operating income is close to zero, so a percentage of it means nothing. Percentages below use **FY2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance midpoint $800M** (range $700-900M; FY2025 Adj. EBITDA about $620M). Q2 2026: revenue $1,443.2M, operating loss -$68.2M, Adj. EBITDA $114.6M (8-K Ex. 99.1: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001883685/000188368526000027/q226-prx8kexx991.htm). Mix (Q2 2026): Sports, including Prediction Markets, 62%; iGaming 32%; Other 6%. Online sportsbook in 27 US states + DC + PR (about 53% of US population). iGaming in 5 US states (CT, MI, NJ, PA, WV; about 11% of population) + Ontario (*verified*, 10-K and 8-K). 2026 Predictions investment is $200-300M (Q2 call, per press summaries).

## Bottom line
- **In play (expected): about +$40M/yr (about 5% of the $800M Adj. EBITDA guidance midpoint; GAAP OI is about $0).** If every favorable outcome happens: about +$213M/yr at maturity. The adverse case (an Ohio sports-betting tax hike) is about -$33M/yr net of mitigation.
- **Locked in regardless:** Illinois per-wager tax (about $70-80M/yr gross pre-mitigation; Pritzker 96.6% to win). NJ rate raised to 19.75% (about $56M/yr, Truist, about half mitigated). Also MD 15→20%, LA 15→21.5%, NC 18→23% (2026), and the end of Colorado's free-bet deduction. The federal 90% gambling-loss deduction cap applies to tax year 2026 unless Congress repeals it.
- **Card fact:** Nebraska votes Nov 3 on letting DraftKings take online sports bets there, but the bigger lever is small: Ohio's governor race, where the Democrat backs legal online casinos.
- **Strongest R-vs-D contrast:** Ohio governor. Amy Acton (D, Kalshi 68.5%) supports legalizing iGaming and pledges no sports-betting tax increase. Vivek Ramaswamy (R, 31.5%) has taken no public position. At the federal level, the gambling-loss-deduction fix and the sports-prediction-market ban bills are both bipartisan, so party control of Congress matters less than the President's signature.

### 1. Nebraska online sports wagering (Nov 3, 2026)
- **Odds:** Kalshi KXSTATEBALLOTMEASURE-NE26, pulled 2026-10-14. OSWA (constitutional amendment) bid/ask 0.60/0.68, vol 73. OSWR (regulation initiative) 0.62/0.71, vol 239. Both are thin. The two measures need to pass together; joint about 0.62 (*estimated*, assuming high correlation). UNL poll, April 2026: 58% support, 31% oppose.
- **Policy:** 20% tax on online sports wagering revenue. 70% of tax goes to property-tax credits. Platforms tie to horse-racing facilities (up to 2 operators per racino). Effective 1/1/2027. Racing & Gaming Commission rules by 6/1/2027.
- **Enactment:** 0.90 *estimated*. The measures are self-executing. The remaining risk is litigation or delay in implementation.
- **Size:** Eilers & Krejcik (commissioned by sponsor Tax Relief Nebraska) estimates just under $87M in state tax over 5 years. At 20%, that is about $435M of GGR over 5 years. LB 421's fiscal note: $9.3M tax in year 1 rising to $20.4M in year 4. At 20%, year 4 is about $102M of GGR. LB 421's rate was not confirmed, so this is *estimated*.
- **Exposure:** DraftKings and FanDuel each gave more than $3.5M to the campaign. DK's share of US online sportsbook GGR is about 34% (third-party). DK's Ohio share is about 31% ($326.9M of $1,045M).
- **Dollars (mature):** `GGR $102M x DK share 0.31 x contribution margin 0.25 (estimated, after 20% tax and marketing) = +$8M/yr`. Expected: `$8M x 0.62 x 0.90 = +$4.5M/yr`. Years 1-2 are likely negative or near zero because of launch promotions (*estimated*).

### 2. Ohio iGaming (governor race)
- **Odds:** GOVPARTYOH-26, 2026-10-14. Acton (D) 0.68/0.69, vol 1,079,799. Ramaswamy (R) 0.31/0.32, vol 1,433,033.
- **Policy:** Acton's campaign is "open to regulating, taxing" online casino, with revenue going to schools, childcare and property-tax relief. She also backs a micro-betting ban and limits on advertising to people under 21. Ramaswamy has no public stance. DeWine (term-limited) opposed iGaming. HB 298/SB 197 missed their 2026 targets.
- **Enactment:** *Estimated* 0.30 under Acton and 0.15 under Ramaswamy, within two years. The GOP legislature has some support (House Republicans backed iGaming in 2024), but casino and local opposition stalled both bills. A governor who signs (D) is more likely than a silent one to move it.
- **Size:** Sponsor Rep. Stewart estimates $400-800M/yr of tax at 28%. That implies GGR of $1.43-2.86B/yr at maturity (midpoint about $2.1B). The LSC fiscal note confirms the 28% rate and $50M licenses.
- **Exposure:** DK iGaming share *estimated* at about 20%. Basis: DK iGaming revenue run-rate of about $1.85B ($461.9M x 4) set against US+Ontario iGaming GGR.
- **Dollars:** `$2.1B x 0.20 x 0.25 contribution margin (estimated) = +$105M/yr` (range `$1.43B x 0.20 x 0.20 = $57M` to `$2.86B x 0.20 x 0.30 = $172M`). Expected: `$105M x (0.685 x 0.30 + 0.315 x 0.15) = $105M x 0.253 = +$26.5M`. The part that depends on who wins is `$105M x (0.30 - 0.15) = $16M` in favor of a D win. Launch would likely be 2028 or later, so the full amount arrives about 2029-30.

### 3. Ohio sports-betting tax hike
- **Policy:** Gov. DeWine proposed 20%→40% in the 2025 budget; the legislature dropped it. Acton has said she would not raise the 20% rate. Ramaswamy has stated no position.
- **Size:** Ohio 2025 taxable sports-betting revenue $1,045.3M. DK $326.9M.
- **Dollars:** `$326.9M x 0.20 additional rate = -$65M gross`. Analysts assume about half is mitigated through promotions (Truist on NJ), giving -$33M. Expected: `-$33M x (0.315 x 0.10 + 0.685 x 0.03) = -$1.7M`.

### 4. Federal gambling-loss deduction cap (P.L. 119-21)
- **Odds:** Kalshi KXGAMBLINGREPEAL (a bill that "removes/changes the gambling tax provision in OBBB"), 2026-10-14:
  - before 1/1/2027: 0.17/0.19, vol 368,472
  - before 4/1/2027: 0.16/0.24, vol 17,206
  - before 9/1/2027: 0.27/0.36, vol 5,124 (thin)
  - before 1/1/2028: 0.37/0.45, vol 3,511 (thin)
  - before 1/1/2029: 0.43/0.50, vol 0
- **Policy:** The cap limits deductions to 90% of losses from TY2026. The FAIR BET Act (Titus, D) failed at House Rules in Jan 2026. Ways & Means then approved a 100% deduction provision 38-5, retroactive to TY2026. The Senate FULL HOUSE Act (Cortez Masto) is in Finance. Support is bipartisan, so the election matters mainly for timing and the vehicle, and the President has not signaled a veto.
- **Size:** JCT: the cap raises about $1.14B over 2026-34 (about $127M/yr of tax on bettors), and full repeal costs about $2B over 2027-36. The American Bettor's Voice advocacy paper claims an industry handle loss of $18B/yr and a $1.5B GGR loss; this is advocacy, not independent.
- **Dollars (*estimated*):** The small JCT base suggests a modest effect concentrated among high-volume and winning bettors. Central estimate is +$20M/yr if repealed. Upper bound: `ABV $1.5B x DK 0.34 x 0.30 flow-through = $153M`. Expected: `$20M x 0.41 = +$8M`.

### 5. Federal ban on sports event contracts
- **Policy:** The Prediction Markets Are Gambling Act (Sens. Schiff D and Curtis R, March 2026; House companion Horsford D and Amodei R, July 2026) would bar CFTC-registered venues from listing sports or casino-like contracts. Trump has publicly favored prediction markets, so a veto is likely.
- **Enactment:** *Estimated* 0.05 under a D sweep and 0.02 otherwise.
- **Exposure:** DK runs both sides. It operates DraftKings Predictions and the DKeX exchange (formerly Railbird, a CFTC DCM), with $200-300M of 2026 investment. It also loses sportsbook handle to Kalshi and others: Citizens estimates about 5% cannibalization ("mid-single-digit").
- **Dollars:** `Sports revenue ~$4.0B (Q2 $891.9M x ~4.5, estimated) x 5% x 0.40 flow-through = +$80M`. Ending the Predictions spend would add up to $200-300M more but give up that business. Expected: `$80M x (0.61 x 0.05 + 0.39 x 0.02) = +$3M` (+$13M if the investment savings are included).

### 6. State treatment of prediction markets
Courts are split. MA state court ruled for state gaming law in January 2026. The 3rd Cir. ruled for Kalshi (preemption) in April 2026. A Utah federal court ruled for the state on 8/5/2026. Washington issued an enforcement order on 8/13/2026. Governors and AGs elected in 2026 will shape enforcement. For DK this cuts both ways: restrictions protect sportsbook GGR (which it pays state tax on) but limit DK Predictions. No defensible size was found, so this is not quantified.

### 7. Other legalization
No other 2026 sports-betting ballot measure exists (Ballotpedia). Missouri launched 12/1/2025 (DK is live there). California's earliest vote is 2028. Georgia needs 120 House votes (got 63 in 2026). Texas meets next in 2027. Minnesota remains stuck on tribal exclusivity. No 2026 race clearly flips any of these. Expected value is about $0 near term (*estimated*).

## Locked in regardless
- **Illinois per-wager tax:** $0.25/bet for the first 20M bets, $0.50 after, on top of a 20-40% graduated GGR tax. Citizens estimated $79M (5.4% of EBITDA) for 2026 before mitigation. DK passes through a $0.50 surcharge per bet. Repeal (HB 5143) died in committee in March 2026. Pritzker is 96.6% (GOVPARTYIL-26, vol 103k) to win, and any repeal would face his veto.
- **New Jersey:** rate raised to 19.75% on online sports and iGaming. Truist estimates about $56M/yr for DK, with about half mitigated in 2026.
- **Maryland:** 15→20%.
- **Louisiana:** 15→21.5%.
- **North Carolina:** 18→23% from 2026.
- **Colorado:** free-bet deduction ended 7/1/2026.
- DK-specific sizes for MD, LA, NC and CO were not found.
- **Federal 90% loss-deduction cap:** in force for TY2026 unless the repeal in channel 4 passes.
- **Governors in DK's largest iGaming and sportsbook states are near-certain holds:** NY Hochul 94%, PA Shapiro 98%, MI Benson 94%, MA Healey 95%, MD Moore 98%, AZ Hobbs 87%, CO Weiser 96%. Tax policy there does not hinge on 2026 control changes.
- **Maine iGaming:** law enacted January 2026, launch expected 2027.
- **Missouri sportsbook:** live since 12/1/2025.

## Biggest uncertainties
1. **Ohio P(enact|governor).** These probabilities are fully *estimated*, and Ohio carries most of the expected value. The GOP legislature, not the governor, is the binding constraint.
2. **Contribution margins (20-30%) in new states.** These are *estimated*. DK does not disclose state-level profit.
3. **Effect of the gambling-loss cap on handle.** The JCT number implies a small effect, while advocacy claims are 10x larger. Kalshi's 2028 contract is thin.
4. **Nebraska Kalshi markets are very thin** (vol < 300). The UNL poll (58-31) is a cross-check.
5. **Prediction markets.** Litigation, not elections, is the main driver, and the net sign for DK is ambiguous.
