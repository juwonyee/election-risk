https://s205.q4cdn.com/882619693/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Ford-Q4-2025-Earnings-Press-Release.pdf).

## Locked in
- CAFE civil penalty set to $0 (P.L. 119-21, July 2025): non-compliance no longer costs Ford fines; credit-purchase need falls.
- 30D/45W EV consumer credits ended Sept 30 2025 (P.L. 119-21).
- Congressional Review Act resolutions (June 2025) revoked the EPA waivers for California's ACC II; litigation pending.
- UAW contract runs to April 30 2028.
- IEEPA tariffs struck down 2026-02-20; Ford booked a one-time $1.3B refund in Q1 2026 (does not repeat).
- The President (R) serves to January 2029: any tariff repeal needs his signature or a two-thirds override.
