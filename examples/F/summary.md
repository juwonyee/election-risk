**Locked in regardless:**
- CAFE civil penalty set to $0 by P.L. 119-21 (July 2025): missed fuel-economy targets no longer cost fines
- 30D/45W EV purchase and lease credits ended Sept 30, 2025 (P.L. 119-21); Model e lost $4.8B in FY2025
- Congress revoked California's ACC II waivers by Congressional Review Act (June 2025); litigation pending
- UAW contract runs to April 30, 2028
- IEEPA tariffs struck down 2026-02-20; Ford booked a one-time $1.3B refund in Q1 2026 that does not repeat
- Section 232 auto, parts and metals tariffs stay unless a veto-proof law repeals them: the President (R) serves to January 2029

**Weakest links:**
- USMCA impact (-$1.3B) is an estimate: ~335k Mexico-built US sales (Maverick, Bronco Sport, Mach-E; 2025 sales partly extrapolated) x ~$25k import value x 25% x ~50% non-US content, plus ~$0.25B for parts. The content share and Ford's ability to price it through drive the range.
- A withdrawal notice is a six-month warning, not withdrawal itself; it may be a negotiating move later withdrawn. Kalshi's thinner KXUSMCAOUTCOMES-27 prices a US withdrawal notice in 2026 at about 13% (last trade, wide spread) versus 6% here.
- Tariff-repeal impact (+$1.0B) uses Ford's own 2026 net tariff cost (CFO: 'better than $1B'); repeal would also help importing rivals, so Ford's relative advantage on US-built trucks shrinks: the net could be smaller.
- House control decides only half of a tariff repeal: the Senate (Kalshi D ~64%) and a presidential veto also stand in the way; the book's 0.05 for a Democratic House already reflects that. Book row for House Democrats' 0.45 'make: alone' (floor disapproval votes) is not used: disapproval resolutions do not apply to Section 232 tariffs, which are what Ford pays.
- California 2035 rule: the EPA waiver is already revoked, so relaxing it matters only if courts restore it; the +$100M is a rough per-vehicle compliance-cost estimate on ~130k California sales.
- Michigan governor (Benson: 'subnational agreements with Canada', book p 0.45) checked and left out: a state agreement cannot change federal tariffs Ford pays, so no dollar effect.
