**Locked in regardless:**
- California SB 623 (signed 2026-06-25): medical-lien damage limits and rideshare safety rules; both competing November initiatives withdrawn
- California Prop 22 upheld: drivers stay contractors with an earnings floor (120% of state minimum wage)
- Massachusetts 2024 AG settlement (contractors with a pay floor) and the 2024 driver-union ballot law
- NYC TLC driver minimum pay and FHV licence cap
- Federal tax changes in P.L. 119-21 through 2028 (below operating profit)

**Weakest links:**
- California AG suit: Uber's cost scales Lyft's $272.5M settlement by Uber's ~3:1 US rideshare share (2-3x used; no California split published) and spreads it over 4 years like Lyft's; it is a one-off for past conduct (2016-2020), not a permanent yearly change, and Uber may already hold part of it in its legal reserves
- Book row doubt, CA-attorney-general.md: the book says the city attorneys of SF, LA and San Diego are co-plaintiffs whoever is AG, yet Gates has no gig-workers row, so his p is 0 and the race swing is 0.70; the suit would likely continue under Gates, so the real swing is smaller
- Book row doubt, FL-governor.md: Jolly's tort-rollback row is framed around property insurance ('so policyholders can sue insurers'); this entry assumes a rollback would also undo the HB 837 negligence changes that lower Uber's auto-liability claims
- PRO Act impact (5-15% of estimated US Mobility + Delivery EBITDA) is an analyst range with no published estimate; the US state split of Uber's profit is not disclosed (all state numbers are population- or revenue-share estimates)
- Uber's total insurance expense is not disclosed; the Florida entry assumes US Mobility insurance of about $4-6B a year
- Book row doubt, OR-legislature.md: SB 1166 is a 2025 bill whose fate the book says is unconfirmed; with Democrats at 0.97 the race barely moves it
- Not linked: NYC $30 minimum wage (KXNYCMINWAGE-27JAN01) - rules price a city wage law, which does not cover contractor drivers and needs state authority; CA Insurance Commissioner rows are home-insurance only
