**Weakest links:**
- Clarity Act +$370M rests on an assumed 10% lift in transaction revenue (range 0-20%); no public estimate of the lift was found (estimated)
- Sports prediction-market link: Coinbase prediction-market revenue ~$100M annualized is a press summary of the Q2 2026 letter, not a filing number; sports share 68-83% from secondary sources
- Book row doubt: US-house/senate-control give the Clarity Act p 0.05 under every majority, while Kalshi prices 21% for law before 2028; the book p and the market disagree by 4x
- Book row doubt: TX-lieutenant-governor Patrick gaming-betting row is an interim study charge (study, then recommend fixes) but is routed 'make: same party, strong' 0.35, while his data-center study charges are 'vague' 0.05; a study charge reads like 'vague'
- Texas link uses Texas's population share (9.2%) for Coinbase's Texas prediction-market revenue; state split not disclosed
- Base is FY2025 operating income $1,435M; H1 2026 was an operating loss, so shares of a 2026 run-rate would be much larger
- Sports-ban market (Polymarket) is thin: $21k volume, 10-25 cent spread
