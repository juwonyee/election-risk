**Risk Officer:** Election risk LOW · Policy risk LOW

- **Election risk LOW:** Nothing passes the bar. Biggest: −$130M a year (−1.8% of profit) if Georgia pauses new data-center construction, but 0% whoever wins.
- **Policy risk LOW:** Nothing passes the bar. Biggest: +$660M a year (+9.1% of profit) if Georgia approves 4 more gigawatts for Georgia Power, but the market is thin (4,000).

**Locked in regardless:**
- Georgia Power base rates frozen through 2028 (allowed return 10.5%)
- The 9,885 MW certified in Dec 2025 (about $16B) is already approved
- P.L. 119-21 cut late wind and solar credits; nuclear credits (45U, 45J) remain

**Weakest links:**
- 4 GW to $660M: rate base per GW from the Dec 2025 certification and a ~10% pre-tax return (estimated)
- Chance a Bottoms administration pauses data centers: 10% (estimated; needs the Republican legislature)
- KXGA4GW trades only a few thousand contracts

**Red Team:**
- Georgia approves 4 more gigawatts for Georgia Power: thin market (4,000); one trader can move the price.
- Georgia pauses new data-center construction: Bottoms's position has no source link, so its chance 0.10 was counted as 0.
