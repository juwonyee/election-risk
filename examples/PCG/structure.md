# PG&E Corporation (PCG): profit structure
Written 2026-10-27 (rewritten from the 2026-10-22 analyst draft; research not redone). Base: FY2025 operating income $4,749M, PG&E Corp consolidated (verified, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/75488/000100498026000009/pcg-20251231.htm)

One reportable segment, 100% in Northern/Central California (5.66M electric, 4.63M gas customers). Revenue is
decoupled from volume: the CPUC (and FERC for transmission) authorizes a revenue requirement, so profit = allowed
return on rate base minus costs the regulator disallows. Profit lines below are rate-base x allowed-return blocks,
not revenue lines. Revenue $24,935M. 2026 guidance: non-GAAP core EPS $1.64-1.66 (reaffirmed Q2 2026,
https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001004980/000100498026000047/pge-q22026pressrelease.htm).

## Where operating profit comes from
| # | Line | $ / yr | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CPUC general rate case (GRC) return: electric distribution, gas, generation. 2026 rate base $54.0B; 2026 revenue requirement $15.4B; 2027 GRC asks $16.6B on $67.0B rate base (decision ~May 2027) | ~$4.1B ($54.0B x 52% x 9.98% ROE = $2.80B equity + $54.0B x 47.5% x 5.04% = $1.29B debt) | 10-K FY2025 p.65-66 (GRC table, 2026 cost of capital), url above | rate base, ROE, weights verified; $ estimated |
| 2 | FERC electric transmission return (formula rate, TO21) | ~$0.8-1.0B (2026 transmission revenue requirement $2.6B, ROE 10.38%, 50% equity; rate base not read) | 10-K FY2025 p.66, url above | revenue req. and ROE verified; return estimated |
| 3 | Diablo Canyon extended operations (to 2029/2030 under SB 846) | ~$100M fixed shareholder payment (escalated); plus ~$230M volumetric at $13/MWh (2,240 MW x 90% CF x 8,760 h), not distributable to shareholders | 10-K FY2025 p.~20, p.66, url above | fixed fee verified; volumetric estimated |
| 4 | Wildfire-mitigation cost recovery outside the GRC (WMCE interim relief ~$500M, TRRRMA ~$380M, WGSC) | ~$0.5-0.9B of 2025 revenue recognized from memo accounts (timing of recovery, not new margin) | 10-K FY2025 p.55, url above | verified |
| 5 | Wildfire Fund expense (amortized shareholder contributions, AB 1054 / SB 254) | -$352M | 10-K FY2025 income statement, url above | verified |
| 6 | Wildfire claims net of recoveries (Kincade, Dixie accruals above insurance) | -$100M (2025); tail much larger | 10-K FY2025, url above | verified |
| 7 | Operating & maintenance (mostly recovered in rates; profit hit only above authorized or if disallowed) | $11,349M cost | 10-K FY2025, url above | verified |
| 8 | Depreciation (recovered in rates) | $4,634M cost | 10-K FY2025, url above | verified |
| 9 | Pass-through energy costs (electricity $2,609M, gas $1,107M, cap-and-trade, public purpose programs) | ~$3.7B cost, zero margin | 10-K FY2025, url above | verified |
| Below OI | Interest $3,028M; income tax benefit $280M; federal NOL $38.3B; 15% CAMT; DOE loan guarantee (Jan 2025) | not in operating income | 10-K FY2025, url above | verified |

| 4, 5, 6, P3, P4 | Wildfire liability standard, Wildfire Fund size and contributions, equity exclusion, recovery of mitigation spend | Legislature + Governor (AB 1054, SB 254, further reform debated 2026); possible ballot measure; CPUC for recovery | state: CA | wildfire-liability |

Base $4,749M FY2025 operating income; the movable lines (rate-base return lines 1-2, ~$4.9-5.1B pre-interest, plus wildfire costs ~$0.45B) touch essentially all of operating profit.

## Locked in
- AB 1054 Wildfire Fund (2019) and SB 254 (2025) are law: $352M/yr fund expense, the 20%-of-equity-rate-base disallowance cap, and the $2.9B equity-exclusion (~-$150M/yr) apply regardless of Nov 3.
- 2026 cost of capital adopted (9.98% ROE, 52% equity) and 2026 GRC revenue requirement $15.4B already authorized.
- SB 846 Diablo Canyon extension to 2029/2030 is law.
