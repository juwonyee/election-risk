**Locked in regardless:**
- AB 1054 Wildfire Fund and SB 254 (2025) are law: $352M a year fund expense, the disallowance cap at 20% of equity T&D rate base, and the $2.9B fire-mitigation equity exclusion (~-$150M a year)
- 2026 cost of capital (9.98% ROE, 52% equity) and the 2026 GRC revenue requirement ($15.4B) are already adopted
- SB 846 keeps Diablo Canyon running to 2029/2030 (~$100M a year fixed payment); neither governor candidate has a stated position on it

**Weakest links:**
- Rate-freeze impact (-$600M): assumes a freeze holds 2027 rates at the 2026 $15.4B instead of the $16.6B requested and that about half of the $1.2B increase is return and depreciation on new plant PG&E cannot cut (estimated; no docket or bill text exists, the pledge is a campaign line)
- Book row CA-governor Becerra rate freeze p 0.30 (route appoint): CPUC commissioners serve staggered six-year terms and a rate freeze on a revenue-decoupled utility needs a CPUC order or statute; 0.30 looks high for a literal freeze, low for a softer 'affordability' cut to the 2027 GRC
- Book row CA-governor Hilton 'break up PG&E' p 0.15 (make: other party): Democrats hold veto-proof supermajorities and the pledge has no mechanism, so 0.15 looks high; the breakup size (-$1.0B = a quarter of the GRC return moving to local owners) is a placeholder share with no source
- Wildfire liability, PG&E's largest state political channel, has no link: both governor candidates are 'vague' at 0.05 and there is no direct market on a liability or Wildfire Fund bill
- Both entries price the same race (KXGOVCA-26); they are alternatives, not additive
