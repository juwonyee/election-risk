Red Team

Weak spots a skeptical reviewer would raise.
  • Predictions spend (+$150M if a federal ban forces DraftKings Predictions to close) is derived by subtraction: management's ~$1B 'core' Adjusted EBITDA vs 2026 guidance of $700-900M. The ban would also end a strategic product; long-run value is not netted
  • Gambling-loss cap: no public estimate of the handle effect was found (Citizens' analyst calls it negative but unquantified). The 0.5-2% of gaming revenue range is the analyst's assumption, not a source
  • iGaming sizes (NY +$125M, OH +$100M, MA +$80M) all use one ratio: PA-like spend per adult x DraftKings' ~17% national iGaming share x ~20-25% EBITDA margin after tax. A new state's tax rate (NY bills have proposed 30%+) could halve these
  • Book row doubted: MA-governor.md gives Healey (who opposes iGaming, block: veto) p 0.07 while Minogue, with no row, gets 0. The candidate who blocks the policy ends up with the higher chance of it; the 0.07 swing points the wrong way
  • Book row doubted: NY-governor.md gives Hochul and Blakeman the same 0.05 (vague) on iGaming, so the race shows no swing although only Hochul controls the budget she has kept iGaming out of
