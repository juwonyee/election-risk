# General Motors (GM): profit structure
Written 2026-10-27 (rewritten from the 2026-10-22 analyst draft; research not redone). Base: FY2025 EBIT-adjusted $12,747M (verified, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1467858/000146785826000011/gmq42025pressreleaseandfin.htm)

EBIT-adjusted is the measure GM steers by. GAAP operating income was $2,909M (verified, 10-K FY2025, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1467858/000146785826000013/gm-20251231.htm), depressed by $7.9B of EV-realignment charges and $0.8B China restructuring, both excluded from EBIT-adjusted. Cross-check: 2026 EBIT-adjusted guidance $14.5-15.5B, 9M 2026 $12,302M (verified, Q3 2026 release, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1467858/000146785826000056/gmq32026pressreleaseandfin.htm).

## Locked in
- P.L. 119-21 (2025 reconciliation law): consumer 30D/45W EV credits ended 2025-09-30; CAFE civil penalties set to $0; 45X phase-down and FEOC limits. Repeal needs the President's signature through Jan 2029.
- Sec. 232 auto (15-25%) and metals tariffs and Sec. 301 tariffs are in force; Congress has no disapproval route. GM guides -$2.5B to -$3.5B gross tariff cost for 2026.
- UAW contract runs to 2028 (private contract, not an election lever).

Base $12,747M. Movable lines touch at least ~$3.5B a year (tariffs gross $2.5-3.5B, compliance $0.4B, incentives $0.27B) = ~25-30% of base, plus undisclosed EV and 45X lines.
