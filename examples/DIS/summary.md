**Locked in regardless:**
- P.L. 119-21: 21% federal rate and FDDEI through 2028 (below operating income)
- Florida minimum wage reached $15 on Sep 30, 2026 under the 2020 amendment; already in the cost base
- Section 301 (10-12.5%) and 232 tariffs in force since 2026-07-24; Congress has no disapproval route
- California's $750M/yr film and TV credit (2025 expansion) is already law

**Weakest links:**
- Federal film credit ~$400M: assumes ~half of the $24B content budget is entertainment, 60% of it produced (not licensed), 55% filmed mostly in the US, 50% of that is US labor (all estimated); competitors get the same credit, so some may be competed away into talent costs
- Film credit market window ends Jan 1, 2027 (26%); the Jul 1, 2027 contract is 52% but trades only 400 contracts. The bill (Schiff/Scott, introduced Sep 2026) has no CBO score or cap found
- Florida Amendment 3 ~$10M: Disney's Florida non-school property tax (~$200M a year) is estimated from 2020-2022 press figures; the cap only helps if Disney's assessments would rise more than 5% a year, and counties may raise millage on non-homestead property to replace the ~$11.9B homestead loss
- Book row FL-ballot.md Amendment 3 gives p 0.70 ('voters: referral' base rate). For a self-executing measure the market price already is the chance, so this file uses it as a direct entry and does not use the 0.70
