# Starbucks (SBUX): profit structure
Written 2026-10-27 (rewritten from the 2026-10-22 analyst draft; no new research). Base: FY2025 GAAP operating income $2,936.6M (verified, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/829224/000082922425000114/sbux-20250928.htm)

FY2025 = fiscal year ended Sep 28, 2025. It includes $892.0M of restructuring and impairments ("Back to Starbucks" store closures), so the clean run-rate is higher. FY2026 (ended ~Sep 27, 2026) is not reported yet; Q3 FY26 year-to-date GAAP operating income was $2,699.3M (https://s203.q4cdn.com/326826266/files/doc_financials/2026/q3/3Q26-Earnings-Release-2026-07-29-FINAL.pdf).

Not government-movable in a direct way: arabica futures, dairy prices, consumer demand, Nestle/PepsiCo terms, FX, D&A.

## Locked in
- California fast-food minimum wage of $20/hr (AB 1228, in force since Apr 2024); further raises go through the Fast Food Council.
- P.L. 119-21 (2025 reconciliation law): federal tax changes in force through at least 2028.
- Coffee exempted from reciprocal tariffs (Nov 2025) and from both 2026 Section 301 actions (60-economy forced-labor tariffs, Brazil 25%); IEEPA tariffs struck down 2026-02-20.
- Starbucks' own US wage floor of $15/hr (since 2022), so state floors at or below $15 do not bind.
- China operations moved to a 40%-owned JV (Apr 2026); China is now royalties + equity income, not segment profit.

Movable lines touch mainly US store labor (~$7-8B of cost), against a $2,936.6M base; tariff exposure is now small because coffee is exempt.
